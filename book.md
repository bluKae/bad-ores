## Ores

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/amadeum.svg" width="32" height="32"> Amadeum

With plate tectonics come interesting side effects, sometimes. This almost crystal-like mineral strangely interacts with... Oh wait, you don't actually care about the mechanics, do you? ... This ore makes funny sounds, and can be worked into decent armor and tools.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/appetite.svg" width="32" height="32"> Appetite

Dude, are you seriously contemplating eating this mineral, whole? It has literally just been burrowed in sand, for who knows how long... Just don't, man. Have some self-respect.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/balancium.svg" width="32" height="32"> Balancium

To make up for long days spent mining 10000 stones, this balances out your stress and frustration. It contains many shinies. What more do you need to know?

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/barely_generite.svg" width="32" height="32"> Barely Generite

Is this a real ore? Or is it just phantastic? Whatever the case, it was only found once before, and is so exceedingly rare that you will probably never be able to make use of it. If you do, please tell us about its properties so we can expand this book!

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/breakium.svg" width="32" height="32"> Breakium

Theoretically, Breakium is just a normal ore that is found below the surface. However, past mining experiments have revealed it to be cursed - All pickaxes get a broken shaft, a crack in the metal, or just straight-up disappear when mining. Handle with care.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/crappium.svg" width="32" height="32"> Crappium

Nobody has ever found a good use for this ore. It's too common to be a commodity, too fragile to make for tools or armor, and just too damn ugly to be purely decorational. You'll be lucky if you don't have to pay to get rid of it.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/crashium.svg" width="32" height="32"> Crashium

This ore has ascended to a higher form of existence. Our trivial matters concern it not, and it has dedicated itself to solving the problems of anti-determinism in the abstract varidimensional multiverse. Good luck simulating that in Minecraft.

### Doesntevenexistium

This is a common ore found deep underground, which serves default practical purposes slightly superior to iron.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/enderite.svg" width="32" height="32"> Enderite

Over the years, some of the Endermen's activity has started to take its tolls over some iron ores, corrupting them with space anormalities. If you suddenly find yourself looking at a giant surface rapidly gaining speed towards your face, they are why.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/explodeitmite.svg" width="32" height="32"> Explodeitmite

Main food source of creepers, this ore contains more explosive power than the famed nuclear weapons. Why does it not obliterate everything, you ask? Well, before the main explosive peak is reached, the material is turned to ashes by its own explosion. Science.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/fleesonsite.svg" width="32" height="32"> Fleesonsite

This ore literally gets up and runs from you when it senses you nearby, crashing head-first into walls, jumping into lava, or running in circles. Consider your own sanity before slaughtering these cute buggers.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/ghostium.svg" width="32" height="32"> Ghostium

You can see it, but you can't grab it. Thus, it was labeled 'ghostium' for obvious reasons - along with similar things like the 'ghostrays' from our sun, or the 'blue-ghost-stuff' that you drink all day.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/idlikeabite.svg" width="32" height="32"> Idlikeabite

Mining this ore is so extremely tedious that you tire from the work and afterwards wonder where the nearest delicious chicken leg was again.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/iwontfite.svg" width="32" height="32"> Iwontfite

Just carrying this ore makes your limbs all wobbly, and your muscles seem to lose all strength. This, dear Steve, is the incredible power of pacifists.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/kakkarite.svg" width="32" height="32"> Kakkarite

This ore is so incredibly powerfully compressed, you have no idea. Like, wow. Mine it, and you will be showered with kakkarite ingots, maybe about 9000 of them.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/killium.svg" width="32" height="32"> Killium

Mining this ore is so amazing, you can guarantee a heart attack afterwards. Just try it yourself - I assure you you will never do anything as great afterwards!

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/lite.svg" width="32" height="32"> Lite

Ohhh, pretty shiny light! Critics say the glow is caused by violent radiation, but seriously, you can't even see that stuff. Jackpot, free light source!

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/lookslikediamondium.svg" width="32" height="32"> Diamond

For whatever reason, there are diamond-impostors around in the ground, too, probably to get the respect of other ores. Many a miner have found themselves at home, holding up their prize from a long day of work, to discover it was actually just Lookslikediamondium.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/marmite.svg" width="32" height="32"> Marmite

All-time favourite bread spread. Contrary to popular belief, it's not organic matter, but actually mined from stone. Try smelting it into its workable form, and enjoy tasty goodness!

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/meteorite.svg" width="32" height="32"> Meteorite

Mining this ore somehow attracts cosmic forces, and makes it rain stone or netherrack. As long as you mine it underground, you should be fine, but remember to warn others above when you do mine it!

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/misleadium.svg" width="32" height="32"> Misleadium

This mineral has widely been reported to give false information to the miner to get spared from harm. The name is misleading, though. While you think the information to be wrong, the item does exist - until you look.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/movium.svg" width="32" height="32"> Movium

You can never quite mine it... Just when you are about done, it just appears to have moved. Maybe you have just mined the wrong spot?

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/nopium.svg" width="32" height="32"> Nopium

Seems to just slip out of your hands, or even your pockets, all the time. Once, a man dedicated his whole life to making himself its master, and crafted a whole armor from them. The result: The armor would somehow just fall off. Maybe you should take this lesson and mine more worthwhile ores.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/nosleeptonite.svg" width="32" height="32"> Nosleeptonite

The ore of your nightmares. The dark king of them all, and otherwise just the incarnation of your deepest fears. AVOID

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/paintitwhite.svg" width="32" height="32"> Paintitwhite

In all actuality, this 'ore' is really just a skeleton ground into little bits by time and tectonics. Maybe it could be used for coloring textiles, or as a fertilizer for your orchis garden.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/pandaemonium.svg" width="32" height="32"> Pandaemonium

Mining this ore rips a hole in the inter-dimensional veils, and lets the nether out into the overworld. 2/10, not recommended.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/polite.svg" width="32" height="32"> Polite

First discovered in the west pole, this ore was labeled 'Polite'. It seems to live a blithesome life underground, often found huddled together in groups. Still, it seems to enjoy being brought into new forms, and has no problem being worked into armor or tools.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/shiftium.svg" width="32" height="32"> Shiftium

Everything passes just right through it. You can't even remove it from its containing stone, much less use it. The only effective way of mining it is with pure energy - explosions!

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/smite.svg" width="32" height="32"> Smite

Smite is said to be Thor's favourite ore. It makes for absolutely awful armor, but maybe you can make some good tools out of it.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/stonium.svg" width="32" height="32"> Stonium

What are you looking this up for. It's just stone. What, are you expecting every rock to look exactly the same?

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/streetscum.svg" width="32" height="32"> Streetscum

Excellent pickpocket, especially considering it shouldn't even be able to move. Try to keep your distance from it.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/tauntum.svg" width="32" height="32"> Tauntum

Some of the noises this mineral makes sound freakishly similar to animals or monsters. Maybe you should mine it just to get rid of distractions.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/unobtainium.svg" width="32" height="32"> Unobtainium

No methods applied to obtain this ore ever worked. Thus, not much is known - but it would make for a goddamn amazing armor, if finally acquired!

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/uselessium.svg" width="32" height="32"> Uselessium

Believe it or not, but this ore is even more useless than crappium. It literally does nothing. Maybe it will go away if you concentrate on ignoring it long enough.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/wannafite.svg" width="32" height="32"> Wannafite

This ore seems to be reluctant to be hit in the head with a pickaxe, thrown into the hot fires of a furnace, and then repeatedly being hammered down into the shape of a tool. It has learned to fight back.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/wantarite.svg" width="32" height="32"> Wantarite

Somehow contains a pig. Don't ask me how, I'm just making these stories up. This is honestly asking too much of me.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/website.svg" width="32" height="32"> Website

This ore has discovered a way to break the 4th wall and found a tunnel out of the box. It slowly discoveres the infinite possibilities outside of Minecraft.

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluKae/bad-ores/main/images/book/zombieunite.svg" width="32" height="32"> Zombieunite

This ore somehow seems to sense the amount of zombies, and becomes less hard to mine with a higher amount. It is therefore not easy to obtain. Protip: Open public facebook event 'Zombie party - everyone invited!'
